# Lumen

A cell's metabolism at a9l.im/lumen: one Canvas 2D plate whose upper panel is the specimen's membrane in cross-section (Goodsell's manner, the cofactor pools as molecules under it) and whose lower panel is the cell's carbon network as a transit map. Both read one stiff kinetic model (`src/engine/`) on a clock from 300× slower than life to 1,000× faster. Vanilla ES modules, no dependencies; KaTeX from jsDelivr renders the reference overlay.

A submodule of the a9l.im repository. The root `AGENTS.md` covers the shared layer, the design system ("Inside the scene" is the authority), build and deployment. `docs/ROADMAP.md` has what's next, the open questions and the model's known approximations.

## Running and checking

Preview through the parent build, or from the working tree with `node tools/sims/serve.mjs 8791` in the parent; the parent's `tools/sims/check.mjs` takes screenshots and reports console errors, overflow and fps.

```bash
node --test 'tests/*.test.mjs'        # headless, Node 22+, no install; about 13 s
for f in main.js colors.js src/*.js src/engine/*.js; do node --check "$f"; done
```

The tests hold only checks whose failure wouldn't show on the plate: the network's balances and conservation laws, thermodynamic consistency around cycles, the integrator's guarantees, and the archetypes' classic behaviours. Add one where a failure would be silent, one per mechanism, never a snapshot of a tuned value.

## The model

- **The engine is authoritative and headless.** Nothing under `src/engine/` touches the page, nothing outside it writes engine state except through cell.js, and nothing reads `store` back into the model, so the chemistry never depends on what's drawn.
- **Species are BiGG ids** with formulas and charges from iJO1366 (iJN678 for RuBP), so balance is checked against an independent source; only the protein carriers and photons are written by hand.
- **Compartments:** `c` cytosol (gases live here), `m` the matrix (animal cell only), `l` the lumen side of the drawn membrane, `e` the medium. Water and buffered H⁺ are fixed; `h_l`, the translocated protons, is a species.
- **Glycogen is a `polymer`** in glucosyl units: it enters saturation but cancels from the mass-action ratio, since a unit added or taken leaves the chain ends as they were.
- **One enzyme's reaction per row**, so opposing enzymes (hexokinase and G6Pase, PFK and FBPase) are separate. FAD stays bound, GTP counts as ATP, and membrane reactions are written per two electrons. `[m]`-style tags place a species in another compartment.
- **Sinks** (the biomass row, E. coli's core composition, and the demand rows) are exempt from balance. Biomass takes its nitrogen as glutamate and glutamine and its lipid as palmitoyl-CoA, so its lumped costs leave out what GS–GOGAT and fatty-acid synthesis spend.
- **Rates are separable**, v = Vmax · activity · η_reg · η_sat · η_thermo, with η_thermo = 1 − e^(ΔG/RT) in the Haldane form: finite at zero concentration, zero at equilibrium, never against ΔG. ΔG includes Δp: each charge moved to the lumen side costs F·Δψ (`z`), each proton F·ΔpH (`nH`).
- **A `brake`** (AtpΘ, CF₁) reads the reaction's own e^(ΔG/RT) and scales both directions, so it stops a stalled synthase splitting ATP without letting any flux run against ΔG.
- **Demand rows** run at their dial's rate, paced by their scarcest precursor (Liebig).
- **Two light switches:** enzymes light turns off use a lower, steeper switch than thioredoxin's, so at dusk the Calvin cycle closes before the night's pathways open instead of both running half on and cycling CO₂.
- **Δψ and ΔpH are separate signed states** (`dq_l` and `h_l`). How Δp divides is set by the counter-ion (`ION`) and K⁺/H⁺ exchange (`KHE`) conductances, and K⁺ is held on both sides.
- **The c-ring** (`ring` per cell) sets ATP synthase's protons per ATP, ring/3; model.js rewrites its equation.

## The integrator

- **ROS2** (linearly implicit Rosenbrock) with a finite-difference Jacobian, stored and factored dense. Rosenbrock steps keep every linear conservation law exactly, and a step that would make a species negative is retried smaller.
- **Steps are error-controlled** (RTOL 2e-3) up to `HMAX`, 60 s, and `H_PULSE`, 0.05 s, while a pulse fades, since a step holds a pulse's factor where it started.
- **Two clocks.** The picture (`engine.t`, `state.x`) is the straight line between the integrator's last two steps, so it also keeps the conservation laws and every sign. A step's size depends only on the state, so a trajectory is the same however frames slice time.
- **`advance(dt, ms)`** stops stepping after `ms` of wall time (main.js gives it 8 ms) and returns the model time it fell short by. The rest is dropped, not owed, and the caption shows the real speed.
- **Outside changes** (state, environment, a dial, a switch, damage, a pulse) happen at the picture's time: `advance` pins the integrator back to it, so a pulse at 300× slower answers at once.
- **Pulses** are functions of model time, so they freeze with the clock and keep trajectories frame-independent. `snapshot()` copies everything but held pulses: it serves the paired baseline, not save and replay.
- **The hold's copies step at most 2 s**, because their fluxes are read on the picture, where a fast enzyme near equilibrium saws with a long step and never settles. Its coefficients are read at stillness (under 0.1%/s), which isn't convergence.
- **Randomness** (rng.js) feeds only the window's sampling, never the chemistry.

## The window

- **Counts are true:** each pool's drawn total is its mean count in a slab the cut's length by 16.5 by 20 nm. The exception is the lumen's protons, one per 2 mV of Δp (`MV_PER`), because almost all translocated protons are bound to buffers.
- **Each drawn complex is one representative centre** driven by its own sampled turnovers (`KCAT` × the share of capacity in use × the clock's speed). An effect is never borrowed from another complex's rate; past 8 a second one turnover in w is drawn and the caption says so.
- **Pool events are sampled at one-way rates**, so an enzyme at equilibrium still turns molecules over. Forward and backward pair up: `d` ±1 changes a molecule and ±0.5 is an exchange (a thread only). `Renderer.mapEvent` must pass `d` through, because rounding it to a sign brings back molecules strobing between forms.
- **A docking complex lets its molecule go as far from the site as it came**, so a busy complex doesn't gather the pool (real pools mix in microseconds).
- **Motion is continuous:** nothing but a new object moves more than about 3 nm a frame, and nothing appears at full opacity.

## Conventions

- A new reaction goes in network.js balanced in elements and charge (a new species' BiGG id goes in tools/bigg.mjs, then regenerate), into the cells that have it, with its ΔG'° in thermo.js and kinetics in rates.js. Then run the tests: an unspecified conservation law, or a cycle with a ΔG'° of its own, is a finding.
- Parameters are illustrative unless a comment cites a source.
- Every effect in the scene reads a quantity in the model and names it in a comment where it is drawn. The accent is the one red and means protons; canvas code reads the palette through `readPalette()` on each theme change.
- The caption is Fig. 1's live values in a line and doesn't repeat what panel a's title, the legend or the key already shows.
- colors.js is a deferred classic script loaded before `main.js`, and its `_freezeTokens()` freezes `_PALETTE`, so new palette keys go above that call.
- `lastUpdated` in src/ui.js, and index.html's title, descriptions and `dateModified`, are rewritten by the parent build from about.md's frontmatter.
- A new membrane complex touches specimens.js (`WIDTH`, `order`, `names`), complexes.js (parts and painter), membrane.js (`drawStatic`, anchor height, hit box, `TOP_NM`, `_stroma`, a docking site if it changes a pool), detail.js, ladder.js, renderer.js (`_ETC_INFO`), info.js (`ENZYMES`), mapbind.js (`COMPLEX`), rates.js (`KCAT`), window.js if its turnovers draw something new, and the network.
- A new map segment touches maplayout.js (`segs`, a station), mapbind.js (`segments`, `STATION`), map.js (`LABEL_AT`, phone overrides, `FLIP`) and renderer.js (`_SEG_INFO`).
