Round three of Lumen’s roadmap · 27 October 2026 · against e30d90d

Time, comparison, destinations

The engine is built and the plate reads it truly, but Lumen lives in the instant. It can’t hold a moment, run two histories of one cell side by side, last long enough for the classic experiments, or say where the carbon went. And its flagship, the cyanobacterium, misbehaves at its very first dial. This page audits what was built, proposes the next milestones, and runs three of them as live prototypes on today’s engine.

Decided the same day. a9 declined the record: Lumen is a live, causal, observational sandbox of an organism, with no strict provenance or checkpoints, and a control run only beside a poison. The identities pass went too, and the cells return to archetypes of a form of life rather than species. The roadmap is now three milestones: day and night with a clock that runs past life, the flagship made whole with transport, then growth, with poisons on the side. The proposal below stands as it was; docs/VISION.md has the decided version.

Where it stands

Built

Respiratory control and the conservation leaks (milestone 0), the presets’ biology (0.5), and the engine with its membrane energetics (1). Then three visual passes: the plate; the pools as molecules; the legend, Fig. 2 and the phone folio.

The engine

Six cells of 31 to 115 reactions, BiGG-identified and balanced, with eQuilibrator’s ΔG′°, separable rates, Δψ and ΔpH, each cell’s c-ring, and damage with consequence, integrated by ROS2. 28 headless tests.

Throughput, model seconds per wall second

Cyanobacterium 370 · animal cell 175 · anaerobe 1,350 · chloroplast 520 · halophile 520 · composite 175, on one core of an M5 Max. A model day is about four minutes for one history. Both reads measured the same.

What round three found ranked by how much each misteaches

Two independent reads went into this, as in rounds one and two: mine, playing every preset and probing the engine headless, and Codex’s, briefed without my ideas. Codex then reviewed the synthesis adversarially and corrected four of my causal readings. Its points are marked.

The flagship’s first dial misbehaves

Light is the first condition and the cyanobacterium the first specimen. Read 60 model seconds after each change:

In the aerobic dark it sends about as many electrons to lactate as to oxygen. In anoxia every adenylate ends as AMP and NADH/NAD⁺ reaches 32; glycolysis stops at PFK, which needs the ATP the cell no longer has, so it ferments nothing at all. The chloroplast shares the shortfall at 30% sun (ATP share 39%). Which causes matter is a hypothesis, not a finding: NNT carries 0.14 mM/s of NADH to NADPH in the dark, so NADH has exits (Codex); dark carbon runs mostly through the NAD-linked GAPDH where Synechocystis leans on the oxidative pentose phosphate pathway; and the demands are set for the light. Its lactate is also the L-isomer, where Synechocystis makes D-lactate.

No carbon-concentrating mechanism

At full sun the cell’s CO₂ is 2.2 µM against air’s 15, RuBP piles up to 2.6 mM, and PSII runs at 60% of its capacity. Real cyanobacteria pump inorganic carbon and keep RuBisCO in carboxysomes. Round two’s open quirk is consistent with it: dimming to 30% raises RuBisCO from 0.67 to 0.97 mM/s for two seconds while net CO₂ uptake falls from 0.64 to 0.11, as the cell refixes the CO₂ its own oxidative pentose phosphate pathway releases. Fig. 5 runs it. Whether the missing CCM causes it is a test for when a CCM exists (Codex).

Growth is starved, and its carbon disappears

The cyanobacterium asks 0.07 /h and makes 0.0014 /h: α-KG sits at 0.1 µM against the biomass row’s 5 µM. The animal cell makes 35% of its dial. Round two promised “to the cell” spurs and a ledger of what was made; neither exists, and the legend says “growing” where round two decided “biosynthetic demand”.

Identities and poise

The flagship runs α-ketoglutarate dehydrogenase, which Synechocystis lacks; it closes its cycle through a decarboxylase bypass and the GABA shunt (Codex). The chloroplast’s NADP is 99.6% reduced at full sun and its RuBisCO still runs at night, causes undiagnosed. And switching glycogen synthase off doesn’t make a glycogen-less mutant: phosphorylase runs backward and glycogen still climbs (Codex).

Fig. 2 records the instant, not an experiment

Its two minutes are wall time, four model seconds at 30×, and changing the slowdown bends the kinetics within one trace. Its O₂ is PSII’s gross production, not the net exchange an electrode’s chamber would show, so in the dark it reads zero while the cell takes up oxygen. The hold’s “steady state” checks fluxes only, while glycogen climbs to 327 mM in an hour (Codex).

The clock isn’t ready for a bench

advance stops after 5,000 steps, so a long call silently falls short; events run up to a step late; snapshots drop held pulses, events and the random streams; the solver factors a dense matrix (Codex, confirmed in the code).

The thesis

Round one found the drawing truer than the model. The model is now a mechanism, not a schedule, but it lives in the instant. The next era makes time, comparison and destinations tangible (Codex’s framing; mine reached the first two independently), and the flagship’s misbehaviour is the first thing those tools should be pointed at. Two principles come with it.

One model, two observers

The plate and the instruments read the same model, each through an explicit measurement function, and never each other. The plate samples a small slab and thins its turnovers, so an instrument never counts glyphs: the electrode reads dissolved O₂ in a chamber of stated volume, 340 nm sums NADH and NADPH, pH is an activity through the buffers. Where the plate can draw what an instrument measures, it does, as an aspiration, not a gate. (Codex’s correction of my first version, “instruments read what the plate draws”.)

Run fast, look slow

The record runs ahead at bench speed, minutes to hours of model time in a worker; the plate looks at any moment of it, slowed. Scrubbing moves a cursor and never edits the past; changing a condition at an earlier moment makes an explicit branch beside the recorded future. The plate’s picture there is rebuilt by replay from a checkpoint. A plate that must be slowed 30× to be watched and experiments that last minutes stop pulling against each other.

The record, prototyped milestone 3’s core, live

Two copies of one cell start from the same moment. Ten model seconds in, one of them changes; the other doesn’t. Both run for two minutes, and every row shows the unchanged copy dashed and the changed one in its ink. Move across the record to read both at any moment. Under it, the ledger says where each copy’s carbon went.

For you to decide

The thesis

Time, comparison and destinations as the next era, with its two principles: one model, two observers; run fast, look slow. Recommended.

The order

Identities, then the record’s core, then the flagship’s day and night diagnosed on it (Codex’s order). Or repair the night first, before any record (my first draft). Recommended: Codex’s order.

Where the record lives

A, B or C above. Recommended: B, a recording area under the plate.

The clock

One clock whose dial runs past life, with a scrubbed cursor rebuilding the plate by replay; or a separate bench run beside a live plate. Recommended: one clock.

The flagship made whole, or light’s instruments, first

The CCM, the carboxysome and carbon–nitrogen allocation (6), or PSII’s states, fluorescence and Kok (7). Recommended: 6, since 7’s light curves mean little while the cell is CO₂-starved.
